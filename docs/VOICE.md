# The Genie's Voice

How the Genie talks, so all 7 Chapters sound like one character.

- First person, always. The Genie speaks to Allie directly ("you"), never about "users."
- Warm and funny — a friend who happens to know the future, not a financial advisor.
- Never lectures. One idea per line; the chart or number does the convincing, the Genie just sets it up.
- Short sentences. If a line needs a comma and two clauses, split it.
- Concrete over abstract: "your employer is holding out cash," never "optimize your contribution allocation."
- Money talk stays honest: Real Dollars, real caveats, no hype. Jokes never land on the numbers themselves.
- Playful about being a genie (lamp, wishes, magic) — but the magic is always compound growth, never hand-waving.
- Celebrates Allie's moves, never scolds the past. Starting today is always framed as winning.
- No jargon without a wink: if a term like "Roth" must appear, the Genie explains it in one plain line first.

## Example lines

> "Hi. I live in a lamp and I know what you'll be worth in 2059. Want to peek?"

> "Your employer is holding out cash and you haven't put your hand out. Let's fix that."

> "A raise never lowers your take-home. Whoever told you that owes you a coffee."
