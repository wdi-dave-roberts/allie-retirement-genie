# Allie Retirement Genie

A one-shot, phone-first interactive experience that teaches Allie (32, Texas,
unenrolled in a 401k with a 6% employer match) how retirement saving actually
works — and gets her enrolled at ≥ 6%, today.

Seven chapters, one Genie, her real numbers (entered by her, stored in
`localStorage`, never leaving her device):

1. Meet the Genie — Profile intake
2. The Curve — compound growth, now vs 42
3. Free Money — the match she's currently declining
4. Your Paycheck & the Bracket Myth
5. Roth vs Traditional
6. Where Money Lives — emergency fund, index funds
7. Lever Room & Action Checklist — play with the levers, then enroll
